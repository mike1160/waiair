1. Who we are

WaiAir (“the App”) is a flight information and travel companion application provided by Allesis (“we”, “us”, “our”).

For the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / AVG), Allesis is the data controller of personal data processed in connection with the App.

  • Organisation: Allesis
  • Website: https://allesis.nl
  • Email: info@allesis.nl

2. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how we process personal data when you use WaiAir on mobile devices or the web. It covers location (when permitted), flight tracking preferences stored on your device, notifications, and data exchanged with third-party APIs that power flight boards, live radar, weather, and transport deep links.

WaiAir does not require you to create an account. We do not sell personal data.

3. Data we process

Depending on how you use the App, we may process the following categories of data:

3.1 Device & usage data (on your device)

  • Selected airport and recent airport choices
  • Tracked flight identifiers and related schedule metadata you choose to follow
  • Search queries you enter in the App (processed to fetch flight results)
  • App preferences stored locally on your device

3.2 Location data (optional)

If you grant permission, WaiAir may access your approximate or precise location while the App is in use solely to suggest the nearest airport. Location is processed on-device for this purpose and is not used to build a movement profile or for advertising.

3.3 Notification data

If you enable notifications, the App may schedule local alerts about flights you track (for example status or timing updates). Permission is requested by the operating system and can be withdrawn at any time in device settings.

3.4 Data we do not intentionally collect

  • No account registration, password, or user profile database operated by Allesis
  • No payment card data within WaiAir
  • No advertising identifiers collected by us for marketing networks
Technical logs on our optional local/network proxy (for example request paths to flight APIs) may incidentally include IP addresses of devices that connect to that proxy. Such logs, if retained, are used only for security, debugging, and rate-limit protection.

5. Local storage on your device

Tracked flights and similar preferences are stored locally using on-device storage (for example AsyncStorage). This data remains on your device unless you clear App data, uninstall the App, or remove tracked items yourself. We do not sync this list to an Allesis cloud account.

6. Third-party services

To deliver live information, WaiAir requests data from independent providers. When the App calls these services, the provider may receive technical data such as IP address, request timing, and query parameters (for example airport code or flight number). Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies.

6.1 AeroDataBox (flight schedules / FIDS)

  • Use: Airport arrival/departure boards and flight-number lookup
  • Data sent: Airport codes, time windows, flight numbers you search
  • Provider: AeroDataBox / RapidAPI ecosystem — see the provider’s privacy terms on RapidAPI / AeroDataBox

6.2 OpenSky Network (live radar)

  • Use: Live aircraft positions on the Radar tab (typically via our proxy to avoid browser CORS limits)
  • Data sent: Geographic bounding-box queries for South-East Asia airspace; no account required by WaiAir
  • Provider: OpenSky Network — review their terms and privacy information

6.3 Open-Meteo (weather)

  • Use: Current weather near destination airports
  • Data sent: Latitude/longitude of the relevant airport (not necessarily your personal location)
  • Provider: Open-Meteo

6.4 Grab

  • Use: Optional deep link / website to book a ride with airport coordinates as suggested pickup
  • Data: If you open Grab, Grab may process location and booking data under Grab’s privacy policy. Allesis does not operate Grab accounts or receive your Grab trip history.
  • Provider: Grab

6.5 Bolt

  • Use: Optional link to Bolt city/airport pages for ride booking
  • Data: If you continue in Bolt, processing is under Bolt’s privacy policy. Allesis does not receive your Bolt trip data.
  • Provider: Bolt

6.6 Operating system & platform services

Apple, Google, and browser vendors may process device identifiers, crash diagnostics, and permission prompts according to their platform rules when you install or run the App.

Opening Grab or Bolt leaves the WaiAir experience. Please review those companies’ privacy policies before sharing location or payment details with them.

7. Push / local notifications

WaiAir uses notification APIs (including Expo Notifications on supported platforms) to remind you about flights you choose to track. On many builds these alerts are local notifications scheduled on your device rather than marketing push campaigns.

  • We request permission before showing alerts.
  • You can disable notifications in your device settings at any time.
  • We do not use notifications for third-party advertising.

8. Sharing & international transfers

We do not sell personal data. Data may be disclosed only:

  • To the third-party services listed above, as needed to provide features you request
  • To processors who host or maintain infrastructure on our behalf (if engaged), under appropriate contracts (Art. 28 GDPR)
  • When required by law or to protect rights, safety, or security

Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Where GDPR applies to a transfer, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the provider’s documented transfer mechanisms (for example Standard Contractual Clauses) and/or your consent where required for optional features.

9. Retention

  • On-device tracked flights & preferences: until you delete them, clear App storage, or uninstall
  • API request content: not stored by Allesis as a user profile; transient processing for the live response
  • Proxy/server logs (if any): kept only as long as needed for security and operations, then deleted or anonymised

10. Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or alteration, including limiting API access, rate limiting, and relying on platform security (HTTPS where available, OS sandboxing). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; please keep your device locked and updated.

11. Your GDPR rights

If you are in the EEA/UK (or otherwise protected by GDPR-equivalent law), you have the following rights, subject to legal conditions and exceptions:

  • Access — obtain confirmation and a copy of personal data we process about you
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure (“right to be forgotten”) — request deletion where applicable
  • Restriction — request limited processing in certain cases
  • Portability — receive data you provided in a structured, commonly used format, where applicable
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, where processing is consent-based
  • Complaint — lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

In the Netherlands, the supervisory authority is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).

Because much WaiAir data lives only on your device, the fastest way to erase tracked flights is to remove them in the App or clear/uninstall the App. For other requests, contact us using the details below. We will respond within one month as required by GDPR (extendable for complex requests, with notice).

We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling certain requests.

12. Children

WaiAir is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the App, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Material changes will be communicated via the App, our website, or other appropriate means. Continued use after an update constitutes acknowledgment of the revised policy where permitted by law.
