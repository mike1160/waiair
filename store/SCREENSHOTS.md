# App Store screenshots

Capture from the iOS Simulator (or a device) at these sizes. Do not upscale.

## Required sizes
| Device | Size | Simulator |
|---|---|---|
| iPhone 6.9" | 1320 × 2868 | iPhone 16 Pro Max |
| iPhone 6.7" | 1290 × 2796 | iPhone 15 Pro Max / 14 Pro Max |
| iPad 13" | 2064 × 2752 | iPad Pro 13" (if you keep `supportsTablet`) |

Marketing composites live in `store/screenshots/` (generated by `node store/generate-screenshots.mjs`).

Replace with Simulator captures for the live listing when you can.

## Overlay style
- Navy `#0A0F1E` top band or gold `#C9A84C` accent line
- San Francisco / Inter, extra-bold
- Keep the live UI fully visible under the headline
- No BETA, no debug text, no empty states
