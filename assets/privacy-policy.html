Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · allesis.nl · info@allesis.nl

1.Who we are

WaiAir is a flight information app provided by Allesis (the Netherlands). For GDPR purposes, Allesis is the data controller.

Website: https://allesis.nl
Contact: info@allesis.nl

2.What we collect

WaiAir is designed to minimise personal data. We do not require an account and we do not sell personal data.

  • Location (optional): only if you grant permission, used solely to suggest the nearest airport while the app is in use. Not used for advertising or profiling.
  • On-device preferences: tracked flights and airport choices stored locally on your device.
  • Search queries: flight numbers or filters you enter, used only to fetch results.
  • No personal profile: we do not collect name, email, payment data, or contact lists inside WaiAir.
Location & notifications are opt-in and can be revoked in system settings.

3.Why we process data (legal bases)

  • Core features (flight boards, search, weather, radar): contract / requested service and legitimate interests in providing a reliable travel tool (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)/(f)).
  • Location & notifications: consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), given via OS permission prompts.

4.Third-party services

To show live information, WaiAir requests data from independent providers. They may receive technical data such as IP address and query parameters (airport code, flight number, map bounds). Their own privacy policies apply.

  • AeroDataBox — airport boards and flight lookup
  • OpenSky Network — live aircraft positions for radar
  • Open-Meteo — weather near destination airports
  • Grab — optional ride deep link / website (processed by Grab if you continue)
  • Bolt — optional city/airport booking pages (processed by Bolt if you continue)

Opening Grab or Bolt leaves WaiAir. Review their policies before sharing location or payment details with them.

5.Push / local notifications

Flight reminders are opt-in only. We request permission before showing alerts for flights you choose to track. You can disable notifications anytime in your device settings. We do not send marketing push from third-party advertisers.

6.Storage & retention

  • Tracked flights and preferences stay on your device until you remove them, clear app data, or uninstall.
  • API responses are processed transiently to display live results; we do not build a cloud user profile.

7.Your GDPR rights

If GDPR applies to you, you may request:

  • Access, rectification, erasure, restriction
  • Data portability (where applicable)
  • Objection to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdrawal of consent at any time
  • A complaint to a supervisory authority (in the Netherlands: Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens — autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl)

Because most WaiAir data lives on your device, deleting tracked flights or uninstalling the app is often the fastest way to erase local data. For other requests, email info@allesis.nl.

8.Children

WaiAir is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9.Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do. Material changes may be highlighted in the app or on allesis.nl.
